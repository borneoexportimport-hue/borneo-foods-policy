1. Introduction

Borneo Food Export and Import Private Limited ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Borneo Foods mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

  • Mobile Number: Used for OTP-based authentication via Firebase.
  • Name and Email: Optionally provided by you in your account profile.
  • Delivery Address: Collected when you place an order.
  • Order Details: Items purchased, payment method, and order history.
  • Payment Information: Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We do not store your card or UPI details.
  • Device Information: Basic device data collected automatically for app functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and manage your account
  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order confirmations and transactional communications
  • To provide shipment tracking via Shiprocket
  • To improve our app and services
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only with trusted third-party service providers required to operate our services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and database
  • Razorpay: Payment processing
  • Shiprocket: Order shipment and tracking

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as required by applicable Indian laws including the GST Act and Consumer Protection Act.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at borneoexportimport@gmail.com.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including Firebase security rules, server-side payment verification, and encrypted data transmission to protect your information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our app is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

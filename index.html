Privacy Policy – Borneo Foods

Last updated: July 2026

Borneo Foods Export & Import Pvt Ltd ("Borneo Foods", "we", "us", "our") operates the Borneo Foods mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, and delivery address, collected when you place an order.
  • Payment Information: Payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI, or banking details on our servers.
  • Order & Usage Data: Purchase history, order status, and app activity to help us process and track your orders.
  • Device Information: Basic device and app diagnostic data used for improving app performance.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To send order updates and OTP verification via SMS
  • To provide customer support
  • To improve our app and services
  • To send promotional offers, only if you have explicitly opted in

Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party providers to operate our app:

  • Firebase (Google): App infrastructure, authentication, and cloud storage
  • Razorpay: Secure payment processing
  • Shiprocket: Order shipping and delivery logistics

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their respective functions and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations.

Data Security

All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

Your Rights

You can access, update, or request deletion of your personal data at any time through the Privacy Center inside the app. You may also withdraw consent for promotional communication at any time without affecting your ability to use core app features.

Children's Privacy

Borneo Foods is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
